NOTE -- machete scope vs. workspace declarations
-------------------------------------------------
cargo-machete inspects each crate's `[dependencies]`, `[dev-dependencies]`,
and `[build-dependencies]` against that crate's source. It does NOT inspect
`[workspace.dependencies]` for declared-but-unused entries because the
workspace table is a versioning shim, not a usage declaration.
