CLAIM UNDER TEST
----------------
No prior public work uses a Cayley-Dickson (sedenion, 16-dimensional)
ASSOCIATOR norm -- the non-associativity defect |(ab)c - a(bc)| of
hypercomplex-embedded magnetometer samples -- as a magnetopause-crossing

SCOPE CAVEAT
------------
Absence of evidence in web search does not prove absence in paywalled,
non-indexed, preprint-embargoed, or non-English literature. Conference
abstracts (AGU, EGU, URSI), theses, and closed-access IEEE/Elsevier
full texts are only partially visible to web search. This dossier
establishes "not found in indexed public web sources as of 2026-07-16,"
not a formal freedom-to-operate or exhaustive systematic review.

OVERALL VERDICT
---------------
NOVELTY NOT FALSIFIED (as of 2026-07-16, indexed public web sources).

No located work: (i) embeds magnetometer or any space-plasma time
series into a sedenion or any Cayley-Dickson algebra; (ii) computes an
associator or associator norm on measured data of any kind; or (iii)
uses a non-associativity defect as a boundary, crossing, or anomaly
detection score. The three ingredients of the claim (sedenion
embedding, associator norm as data statistic, magnetopause detection)
each live in separate literatures that do not cite one another.
