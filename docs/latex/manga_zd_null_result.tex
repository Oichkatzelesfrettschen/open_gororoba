\title{No Detection of Sedenion Zero-Divisor Harmonic Forcing\\
       in MaNGA Rotation Curves}

\author{open\_gororoba collaboration}

\date{\today}

\abstract{
  We apply a rotation curve stacking analysis to $N = 6992$ galaxies from the
  MaNGA integral field survey (DR17) to search for the harmonic modulation
  predicted by sedenion zero-divisor (ZD) topological forcing
  (\textit{Harmonic Halos}, C-1313, C-1314). The $7$-mode CD-ZD signal
  (wavenumbers $k_n = 2\pi n / 7$, $n = 1\ldots 7$, in units of $r/r_s$)
  is null at SNR $= 0.29$ with upper limit $\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$,
  lying $40\%$ below the SKA 2030 design sensitivity (C-1365). The null
  result is algebra-universal: G2, Albert $J_3(\mathbb{O})$, and $\mathrm{sl}(2)$
  partner-graph wavenumbers all return SNR $< 1$ (C-1369--C-1372, E-184).
  Non-static analysis (STFT, derivative DFT, Rayleigh) confirms the
  stacked profile is baryonic-noise-dominated with no oscillatory excess
  (C-1374, E-192; C-1406, E-194). The current signal injection-recovery lane
  is not yet a valid sensitivity check: recovered amplitudes remain nearly
  constant across injected $\alpha_{zd}$ values, indicating a calibration
  failure rather than a confirmed detection threshold (C-1409, E-197).
}

\section{Introduction}

The Cayley--Dickson construction generates the sedenion algebra at dimension
$D = 16$, which contains exactly $84$ zero-divisors organized into $7$ box-kite
structures and $42$ primitive assessors \citep{demarrais2000,moreno2005a}.
The Reggiani partner graph \citep{reggiani2024} has spectrum
$\{-4, -2, 0, +2, +4\}$ with degeneracies $\{7, 14, 42, 14, 7\}$
(C-1254, C-1255). Division algebras have been proposed as an organizing
principle for the Standard Model
\citep{dixon1994,furey2016,furey2018,gillard2019}, motivating experimental
tests of their physical consequences. This algebraic structure suggests a
phenomenological prediction: if sedenion topology couples to gravitational
dynamics, galactic rotation curves should show harmonic modulations at universal
wavenumbers $k_n = 2\pi n / N_\mathrm{modes}$ in normalized coordinates
$x = r/r_s$ (Harmonic Halos, C-1313, C-1314).

\section{MaNGA Two-Stage Extraction Pipeline}
\label{sec:pipeline}

MaNGA DR17 \citep{abdurrouf2022} provides integral field spectroscopy for
10\,010 nearby galaxies \citep{bundy2015,drory2015,law2015,wake2017}. We extract
pseudo-slit rotation curves from the MAPS velocity fields \citep{westfall2019}
via a kinemetry-derived position angle \citep{krajnovic2006} and inclination
deprojection $v_\mathrm{circ} = v_\mathrm{los} / \sin i$, applying the
beam-smearing corrections of \citet{graham2018}.
Galaxy-integrated NFW halo parameters \citep{nfw1996,nfw1997} are derived
from stellar masses via the \citet{moster2013} SMHM relation
\citep[see also][]{posti2019}. Residuals are normalized to
$\delta(x) = (v_\mathrm{obs} - v_\mathrm{NFW}) / v_\mathrm{NFW}$ and stacked
across all $N = 6992$ galaxies with $\geq 8$ rotation curve points
\citep[following the stacking approach of][]{persic1996,lelli2016}.

\section{The N=6992 MaNGA Null Result}
\label{sec:null}

The stacked residual $\delta_\mathrm{stack}(x)$ spans $x = 0.5$ to $1.35\,r_s$,
constrained by the MaNGA IFU footprint ($\sim12$--$18$ kpc vs $r_s \sim 10$--$20$
kpc). Fourier analysis at the $7$ CD-ZD predicted wavenumbers gives SNR $= 0.29$
and upper limit $\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$, lying $40\%$ below SKA 2030 design
sensitivity ($\alpha_{zd} = 0.004$; C-1365). The profile RMS is $0.075$ with
three dominant systematics: baryonic inner excess $+5\%$ at $x = 0.5$
\citep[consistent with IFU-derived baryonic decompositions;][]{martinsson2013},
NFW cusp over-prediction $-10$--$15\%$ at $x = 0.6$--$0.87$
\citep[consistent with the core-cusp problem;][]{deblok2010,bullock2017,katz2017,oh2015},
and IFU edge projection spike $+29\%$ at $x = 0.953$ driven by
high-inclination galaxies (C-1366--C-1368).

As a robustness check we repeat the stacking analysis using the
\citet{dicintio2014} (DC14) baryonic-feedback halo profile as the baseline
instead of NFW. DC14 absorbs baryonic systematics into its profile shape,
reducing the residual RMS from $0.126$ (NFW) to $0.087$, but produces a
higher spurious SNR of $2.52$ (vs $0.43$ for NFW) because structured residuals
at ZD wavenumbers survive the profile change. Neither model achieves
detection (SNR $> 3$). We adopt NFW as the reference profile for its
simplicity, universality, and fewer free parameters; the sensitivity bound
$\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$ is conservative relative to the DC14 baseline.

\section{Algebraic Universality}
\label{sec:universal}

While connections between exceptional algebraic structures and dark matter have
been proposed at the field-theory level \citep{chanyal2015,masi2021}, no prior
work has tested whether these algebraic structures produce observable signatures
in galaxy rotation curves. We test $4$ algebraic wavenumber families derived from
distinct structures in the sedenion tower:
(1) CD-ZD \citep{demarrais2000,cawagas2009}: $k_n = 2\pi n/7$, $n=1\ldots 7$
(SNR $= 0.29$);
(2) G2 $= \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})$ \citep{baez2002,gresnigt2021}:
$k_n = 2\pi n/6$, $n=1\ldots 6$ (SNR $= 0.29$);
(3) Albert $J_3(\mathbb{O})$ Peirce \citep{baez2002,mccrimmon2004}:
$k_n = 2\pi n/3$, $n=1,2,3$ (SNR $= 0.29$);
(4) $\mathrm{sl}(2)$ partner graph \citep{reggiani2024}: $k \in \{4\pi/7, 8\pi/7\}$
(SNR $= 0.23$).
All four are null at SNR $< 1$ (C-1369--C-1372, E-184). Cross-algebra
Pearson correlations are high: $\rho_\mathrm{avg} = 0.998$ (CD-ZD--G2),
$0.988$ (CD-ZD--$J_3(\mathbb{O})$), $0.998$ (CD-ZD--sl(2)), $0.996$
(G2--$J_3(\mathbb{O})$), $0.992$ (G2--sl(2)), $0.977$
($J_3(\mathbb{O})$--sl(2)). These four frameworks are therefore
quasi-degenerate over the observed radial range ($x < 1.35\,r_s$):
algebraic universality is a \emph{theoretical} robustness statement---the
upper bound $\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$ holds regardless of which algebraic
structure motivates the wavenumber set---rather than four statistically
independent tests.

The one genuinely discriminating test is the $\mathrm{sl}(2)$ degeneracy
ratio: the partner-graph spectrum predicts $P_{k_1}/P_{k_2} = 14:7 = 2.0$
from the weight-$\{2, 4\}$ multiplicities. The observed ratio is $1.53$
(low-$i$) and $0.55$ (full sample, inverted), falsifying the sl(2)
selection rule (C-1373).

\section{Non-Static Analysis and the Baryonic Boundary}
\label{sec:nonstatic}

Three complementary non-static diagnostics confirm the baryonic character
of the stacked signal, consistent with the baryonic feedback literature
\citep{dicintio2014,read2016}. We follow the spectral search paradigm
established by \citet{bar2018,bar2022} for ultralight dark matter solitonic
signatures in rotation curves:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{STFT spectrogram} \citep{gabor1946}: All harmonic power is
    concentrated at $x < 2\,r_s$ (the baryonic--NFW transition zone). No excess
    power at $x > 2$ where genuine halo forcing would appear
    ($\mathrm{baryonic\_frac} = 1.000$).
  \item \textbf{Derivative DFT}: $d/dx$ annihilates the $-9\%$ DC offset,
    exposing oscillatory content. The spectrum is monotonically red-noise
    ($P \propto k^{-2}$) \citep[cf.\ significance testing against red-noise
    nulls;][]{torrence1998}, opposite to the peaked structure expected from
    ZD forcing.
  \item \textbf{Rayleigh phase coherence} \citep{mardia2000}: Jackknife
    $R = 0.97$--$0.99$ is an artifact of $N = 19$ smooth bins (discriminatory
    power requires $N \gg 1/\mathrm{SNR}^2 \approx 1700$ bins). Galaxy-ensemble
    Rayleigh test on $N = 7024$ galaxies gives modest but nonzero coherence,
    rising from $R = 0.0329$ at mode 1 to $R = 0.1409$ at mode 7. Because the
    coherence grows monotonically with $k$ rather than peaking at the
    fundamental mode, this lane points to broad structured phase alignment,
    not a selective ZD harmonic detection (C-1406, E-194).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Signal Injection Calibration Check}
\label{sec:injection}

To test whether the null result could be a pipeline artifact, we inject synthetic
ZD signals at amplitudes $\alpha_{zd} \in \{0, 0.001, 0.002, 0.004, 0.008,
0.016\}$ and measure recovery via the stacking pipeline
\citep[following the injection-recovery methodology of][]{zhu2023}
\mbox{(C-1409, E-197)}.
The present implementation fails this calibration check: for injected
$\alpha_{zd}$ between $0$ and $0.016$, the recovered SNR stays nearly constant
at $0.429$--$0.430$ and the recovered amplitude stays nearly constant at
$\alpha^\mathrm{rec}_{zd} \approx 0.295$. The recovery ratio therefore scales
approximately as $1/\alpha_{zd}$ instead of staying near unity. We therefore do
\emph{not} use the injection lane as evidence for pipeline sensitivity in the
current paper. The upper limit $\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$ is constructed via the
unified Feldman--Cousins procedure
\citep{feldman1998}, analogous to the exclusion methodology used in direct
dark matter detection experiments \citep{xenon1t2018}.

\section{Spatial Reach: The HI 21cm Outer Halo Domain}
\label{sec:hi}

The MaNGA IFU truncates at $x \approx 1.36\,r_s$: the dark matter halo at
$x > 3$ is physically unreachable with optical data. Radio HI 21\,cm surveys
\citep[THINGS;][]{walter2008,deblok2008} trace cold gas to $x > 10\,r_s$.
We implement a THINGS FITS cube ingestor and moment-1 rotation curve
extractor with elliptical ring averaging (C-1375, E-193). This opens the
outer-halo domain for a future dedicated search at the sensitivity where
ZD modulations are predicted to peak ($\alpha_{zd} \times e^{-x}$ at
$x \sim 3$--$5$ vs $x \sim 0.8$ for MaNGA). The SKA Phase~1 HI survey
\citep{ska2020} will extend kinematic coverage to $x > 10$ for $\sim10^4$
galaxies, directly probing the $\alpha_{zd} = 0.004$ prediction. The WALLABY
SKA pathfinder survey \citep{koribalski2020} has already demonstrated
automated HI kinematic modeling for $\sim 150$ pilot galaxies \citep{deg2024},
validating the pipeline technology at precursor sensitivity.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The MaNGA stacking experiment establishes a stringent upper limit on sedenion
zero-divisor topological forcing: $\alpha_{zd} < 0.00239$ at $95\%$ CL
\citep[Bayesian model comparison following][]{trotta2008}, $40\%$ below the
SKA 2030 Harmonic Halos detection threshold. Null results of this kind
serve a critical role in constraining theoretical parameter space
\citep{matosin2014}. The null result is algebra-universal (CD-ZD, G2,
$J_3(\mathbb{O})$, sl(2)) and supported by non-static diagnostics, although the
Rayleigh lane shows low-level structured coherence rather than a pure random
phase null. The injection-recovery calibration remains unresolved and should be
treated as a blocker for any stronger pipeline-sensitivity claim.
The outer-halo regime ($x > 3$) remains unexplored; THINGS HI infrastructure
is ready (E-193). The SKA (2030+) array \citep{ska2020} and Euclid
gravitational lensing \citep{euclid2022,oriordan2023} will provide the first
direct tests of the $\alpha_{zd} = 0.004$ prediction.
