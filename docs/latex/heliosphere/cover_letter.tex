\textbf{Summary of revisions.}
The revision addresses three major concerns raised by the reviewers:

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textit{Embedding-lag sensitivity (MC1).}
We added a full tau-sweep experiment (Section~3) evaluating lags
$\tau = 1, 2, 5, 10$~min on the THEMIS-A Staples+2020 magnetopause catalog.
Performance peaks at $\mathrm{F1}=0.601$ ($\tau=1$~min), degrades to
$\mathrm{F1}=0.431$ at $\tau=2$~min, and collapses at $\tau \geq 5$~min.
The monotonic decay directly validates the physical crossing-timescale
argument: a 1-minute lag produces an 8-minute embedding window that matches
the 5--20~minute magnetopause transit time, while longer lags integrate
across pre- and post-boundary plasma simultaneously.

\item \textit{Alfvenic selectivity and micro-switchback correlation (MC2).}
We added two new experiments.
(a) An Alfvenic control analysis on PSP Encounter~3 (Section~4) shows the
CD associator fires $2.83\times$ more frequently during compressive
intervals than during Alfvenic intervals (gradient: $2.91\times$),
confirming topological selectivity rather than amplitude sensitivity.
(b) A micro-switchback correlation analysis on the same interval (Section~5)
finds quiet-interval CD fires are $1.72\times$ enriched near consecutive
B-vector micro-rotation events ($\geq 15^\circ/\mathrm{min}$), yet
32.5\% of quiet fires have no adjacent rotation event, establishing
sensitivity to sub-$\mathbb{R}^3$ topology.

\item \textit{Data gaps, spin-tone filtering, and epsilon regularization (MC3).}
We quantified data-gap fractions (THEMIS 47/7{,}968~min; MMS 14/9{,}460~min),
added a Dirichlet-kernel calculation demonstrating $>46$~dB attenuation of
spin-tone harmonics above 0.5~mHz by the FIELDS search-coil bandpass,
and provided a physical justification for the epsilon noise-floor
regularization as a phase-transition mechanism at the Alfv\'en critical
surface.

\end{enumerate}
