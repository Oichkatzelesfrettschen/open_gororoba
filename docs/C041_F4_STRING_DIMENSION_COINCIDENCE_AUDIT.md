# C041 F4 String Dimension Coincidence Audit

C-041 tested whether the 26-dimensional representation associated with F4
explains the bosonic-string critical dimension `D = 26`. The registered result
is refuted.

## Claim

The rejected claim was that the F4 26D representation and the bosonic-string
critical dimension share a causal mechanism because the numbers agree.

## Audit Result

The equality of the numbers is not enough. The two dimensions arise from
different mechanisms:

- The F4 value names a representation dimension in exceptional Lie algebra and
  Jordan-algebra structure.
- The bosonic-string value is the critical spacetime dimension required by
  Weyl anomaly cancellation in the bosonic string.

No registered derivation maps the F4 representation count into the string
worldsheet anomaly calculation. The correct status is therefore refuted as a
numerical coincidence.

## Falsifier

The conjecture would need a derivation that passes through the worldsheet
central-charge or Weyl-anomaly calculation and returns the F4 representation as
the controlling physical degree count. A representation-dimension match alone
does not meet that burden.
