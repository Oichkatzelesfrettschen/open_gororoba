Note on AMDA vs SPDF
--------------------
T-058 description specifies "Bartol vs AMDA" cross-validation.  The
existing comparator binary (crates/gororoba_cli_data/src/bin/
bartol_spdf_crossval.rs) compares Bartol vs SPDF, where SPDF .asc
files at data/external/voyager2/vy2_YYYY.asc are sourced from
spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov per crates/data_core/src/catalogs/voyager.rs:23.
SPDF and AMDA both ultimately derive from NSSDC level-2 data; for
1990-1995 V2 data the SPDF path is the locally-available proxy.

Key findings
------------

1. SPDF .asc files at data/external/voyager2/vy2_1990..vy2_1995.asc
   contain ZERO finite B-field magnitude rows for 1990-1995 (SPDF
   fill-fraction 0.44 for B but 0 finite-pair overlap with Bartol's
   100% fill).  This is the dataset the comparator reads.  The B-field
   cross-validation cannot be completed against this SPDF source for
   1990-1995; either (a) AMDA provides the missing B-field coverage,
   or (b) a different SPDF file (e.g. magnetometer-only product) is
   needed.

2. Bulk speed cross-validation is excellent: r = 0.9994, RMSE
   1.6 km/s, mean bias well within 1% of the ~465 km/s mean.  This
   confirms the two providers agree on plasma bulk velocity through
   the entire 1990-1995 V2 window (Voyager 2 was at ~31 to 47 AU
   during this period).

3. Proton density cross-validation is weak: r = 0.27, with SPDF
   reporting ~10x larger mean density than Bartol (0.039 vs 0.004
   cm^-3).  Likely a unit-convention or scaling discrepancy in the
   density columns; needs separate investigation.

Recommended next-action
-----------------------

* Status of E-128: PARTIAL.  Plasma cross-validation done; B-field

* Acquire AMDA-specific magnetometer files for V2 1990-1995 and

* Investigate the proton density discrepancy as a separate ticket;
