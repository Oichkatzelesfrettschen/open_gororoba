## PART 5: CITED GAPS & LIMITATIONS

### From Ruan & Fan (0909.3323, Foundational)
- Gap: Limited to small obstacles or cylindrical/spherical symmetry
- Gap: Single angular momentum channel analysis
- Left for future: Coupling to multiple channels; arbitrary shaped scatterers

### From Feshbach Framework (Gallinet & Martin 1105.2503)
- Gap: Arbitrary geometries require numerical validation
- Limitation: Material losses must be incorporated phenomenologically
- Gap: No explicit treatment of dissipation mechanisms

### From Worldline QFT Papers (gr-qc/0412095, 0710.5572)
- Gap: Higher-loop calculations not developed
- Gap: Curved spacetime backgrounds (beyond constant EM fields)
- Limitation: Computational cost grows with diagram complexity

### From PT-Symmetric Scattering (Kristensen et al. 1701.02929)
- Gap: Non-Hermitian loss-gain coupling not fully explored in TCMT context
- Limitation: Quasinormal modes less intuitive than conventional modes

### From BIC Research (Bogdanov 1805.09265, Abujetas 1901.03122)
- Gap: Nonlinear BIC dynamics (Kerr/two-photon) unexplored
- Gap: Loss-induced BIC decay mechanisms (Q-factor limitation)
- Limitation: Fabrication tolerances still challenging for ultra-high-Q

### From Nonlocal STCMT (Overvig et al. 2307.01186)
- Gap: Full dispersion inclusion in TCMT (frequency-dependent coupling)
- Gap: Multiple coupled resonances in nonlocal regime
- Limitation: No closed-form STCMT for general spatial profiles

### From Cosmological Mixing (Anninos et al. 2401.08346)
- Gap: Graviton-photon oscillations in strong-field gravity
- Gap: Backreaction on spacetime geometry
- Limitation: Conformal invariance restricts oscillation mechanisms

### From Recent Photon-Graviton Work (Ahmadiniaz et al. 2601.23279)
- Gap: Three-loop photon-graviton amplitudes (only one-loop computed)
- Gap: Realistic astrophysical/cosmological frequency scales
- Gap: Experimental detection signatures

## PART 7: UNSOLVED PROBLEMS & GAPS

### Problem 1: Photon-Graviton TCMT Coupling
**First Identified**: Implicit in Ruan-Fan (0909.3323) + Bastianelli papers
**Current Status**: No unified TCMT for photon-graviton mixing exists
**Feasibility**: HIGH (2-3 sprint estimate)
**Why Impactful**: Would bridge quantum field theory and resonant scattering
**Dependencies**: Complete worldline amplitude (E-073 done), TCMT framework (C-823)
**Relevant Papers**: gr-qc/0412095, 0710.5572, 2601.23279 (QFT side) + 0909.3323 (TCMT side)

### Problem 2: Nonlinear TCMT for Bound States in Continuum
**First Identified**: Bogdanov et al. (1805.09265), Abujetas et al. (1901.03122)
**Current Status**: Linear BIC theory mature; nonlinear (Kerr) BIC incomplete
**Feasibility**: MEDIUM (3-4 sprints)
**Why Impactful**: BICs enable ultra-high-Q; nonlinear effects critical for power handling
**Limiting Paper**: Bogdanov et al. (1805.09265) calls out nonlinear TCMT gap
**Relevant Papers**: 1901.03122, 1805.09265, 2303.12264

### Problem 3: Loss Mechanisms in Ultra-High-Q Resonances
**First Identified**: Multiple papers (BIC decay, nonlocal loss suppression)
**Current Status**: Phenomenological Q-factor formulas; no predictive loss model
**Feasibility**: MEDIUM (3-4 sprints)
**Why Impactful**: Q-factor ultimately limited by material and radiative loss
**Relevant Papers**: 2303.12264, 2307.01186, 2502.03077

### Problem 4: Symmetry-Broken Fano in Nonlocal Media
**First Identified**: Overvig et al. (2307.01186), Ge et al. (2502.03077)
**Current Status**: Nonlocal metasurfaces demonstrated; TCMT extension partial
**Feasibility**: MEDIUM (2-3 sprints)
**Why Impactful**: Band folding can suppress traditional symmetry requirements
**Relevant Papers**: 2502.03077, 2307.01186, 2505.00396

### Problem 5: Graviton-Photon Oscillations in Strong-Field Gravity
**First Identified**: Anninos et al. (2401.08346)
**Current Status**: Weak-field FLRW analysis only; strong-field unexplored
**Feasibility**: VERY HIGH (requires new formalism)
**Why Impactful**: Cosmological gravitational waves plus EM coupling
**Relevant Papers**: 2401.08346, gr-qc/0412095, 2601.23279

### Problem 6: Three-Loop Photon-Graviton Amplitudes
**First Identified**: Bastianelli & Schubert (gr-qc/0412095, implicit)
**Current Status**: One-loop complete (2601.23279); two/three-loop absent
**Feasibility**: VERY HIGH (computational)
**Why Impactful**: Higher-loop corrections essential for precision physics
**Relevant Papers**: gr-qc/0412095, 0710.5572, 2601.23279

### Problem 7: Fano-BIC Transition in Realistic Lossy Media
**First Identified**: Bogdanov et al. (1805.09265), Abujetas et al. (1901.03122)
**Current Status**: Mostly lossless theory; loss treated perturbatively
**Feasibility**: MEDIUM (2-3 sprints)
**Why Impactful**: Real fabrication has unavoidable material loss
**Relevant Papers**: 1901.03122, 1805.09265, 2303.12264, 2502.03077

## PART 10: KEY INSIGHTS & SYNTHESIS

### Evolution of TCMT Framework
1. **2009 (Ruan-Fan)**: TCMT introduced for angular momentum channels; scattering/absorption cross-sections
2. **2011-2017**: Extensions to arbitrary geometries (plasmonic, nonlocal); alternatives (quasinormal modes)
3. **2018-2019**: BIC connection; destructive interference; Q-factor divergence
4. **2023+**: Symmetry constraints; energy conservation limits; nonlocal spatial effects
5. **2025+**: Generic constraints on TCMT from first principles (Maksimov); complete photon-graviton amplitude

### Critical Unsolved Bridge: Photon-Graviton TCMT
- **QFT side (2004-2026)**: One-loop worldline photon-graviton amplitude complete (2601.23279)
- **TCMT side (2009-2025)**: Generic photonic TCMT mature; constraints from symmetry (2505.00396)
- **Missing**: Unified TCMT for photon-graviton mixing with clear asymmetry parameter q_grav
- **Impact**: Would enable precise predictions of gravitational dichroism, absorption cross-sections

### Practical Near-term Gains
1. **Nonlocal Fano control** (2502.03077): No symmetry breaking needed; band folding generically produces Fano
2. **Lattice-engineered Q** (2303.12264): 14.6x enhancement via unit cell design; fabrication-robust
3. **BIC Q-factor limits** (1901.03122): Inverse proportional to detuning; tuning laws clear

### Long-term Frontiers
1. **Nonlinear TCMT**: Kerr-BIC bifurcations; power-dependent Q-factor
2. **Strong-field gravity**: Remove cosmological conformal invariance; astrophysical signals
3. **Three-loop gravity**: Complete next-order QFT corrections
