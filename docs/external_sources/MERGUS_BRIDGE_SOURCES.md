# Mergus Bridge Sources

Focused source bundle for RFC-001.1 "The Mergus Bridge".

## Scope

These are the core mathematical sources used to justify the repo's revised
architecture: a strong Cayley-Dickson kernel, a classically grounded
structurable middle tier, a Jordan-pair geometry layer, and a symmetric
composition / triality route for exceptional constructions.

## Primary sources

1. R. D. Schafer, "On the algebras formed by the Cayley-Dickson process."
   Kernel-side law class reference for the post-octonionic tower.
2. B. N. Allison, "A class of nonassociative algebras with involution
   containing the class of Jordan algebras."
   Structurable-algebra framework.
3. B. N. Allison and J. R. Faulkner, "A Cayley-Dickson process for a class of
   structurable algebras."
   Classical bridge from a doubling process into the structurable world.
4. O. Loos, "Jordan Pairs."
   Canonical pair-theoretic route from algebra to projective and incidence
   geometry.
5. K. McCrimmon, "The Freudenthal-Springer-Tits constructions of exceptional
   Jordan algebras and the structure of the exceptional simple Lie algebras."
   Standard exceptional Jordan path.
6. A. Elduque, "The magic square and symmetric compositions."
   Symmetric-composition route for triality and magic-square constructions.
7. A. Elduque, "The magic square and symmetric compositions. II."
   Para-Hurwitz and split real-form continuation of the same route.

## Repo-facing reading

- Use Allison and Allison-Faulkner to justify `gororoba_structurable` as a
  first-class bridge lane rather than an ad hoc adapter.
- Use Loos to keep the geometry bridge pair-theoretic instead of matrix-only.
- Use Elduque to justify adding Okubo / para-Hurwitz / explicit triality APIs
  alongside the ordinary Hurwitz/CD kernel.
