# ENSO and local precipitation outlook for 91103

## Result

The August 2026 NOAA forecast suite supports a strong seasonal wet tilt for
Pasadena during the 2026-27 cold season, with the most credible local signal
arriving after autumn rather than immediately.

- The official ENSO discussion places the chance of a very strong 2026-27 El
  Nino above 90 percent and gives a 69 percent chance that OND RONI reaches at
  least +2.5 C.
- The official phase table assigns a 100 percent El Nino probability to SON,
  OND, DJF, and JFM.
- The official long-lead discussion favors a delayed onset of the main southern
  wet signal. OND probabilities remain generally 33-50 percent across the
  southern tier. Probabilities exceed 50 percent across much of coastal
  California from DJF through FMA, with the broadest coverage during JFM.
- The September 1 NWS Pasadena forecast remains mostly sunny and dry through
  Friday. A limited shower chance enters during the weekend. ENSO does not
  replace the short-range forecast.

`reports/enso_91103_source_snapshot_2026-09-01.toml`. The generated-form report
is retained in `reports/enso_91103_outlook_2026-09-01.toml`.

remain decisive.

## Repository implementation

Run the bounded experimental target with:

```text
cargo run -p gororoba_cli_data --example enso_local_outlook -- \
  --input reports/enso_91103_source_snapshot_2026-09-01.toml \
  --out reports/enso_91103_outlook_2026-09-01.toml
```

The target performs four operations:

1. Validate the source snapshot, probability ranges, RONI quantile ordering,
   season uniqueness, and local climatology fields.
2. Hash the complete source snapshot and carry the source records into the
   emitted report.
3. Emit the official ENSO state and a conservative local precipitation
   interpretation without manufacturing a ZIP-scale rainfall total.
4. Stage `CD-ENSO-ISSUE-S16-v1`, a 16D sedenion forecast-state diagnostic.

The 16D coordinate layout is fixed as four seasons in the order SON, OND, DJF,
JFM, with four features per season:

```text
RONI median / 3.5
RONI 90-percent interval width / 3.5
El Nino probability / 100
local above-normal precipitation probability floor / 100
```

The full vector is unit-normalized. Three successive monthly issue vectors are
the mathematical minimum for one associator. Only one complete issue is
committed here, so the diagnostic returns `insufficient_issue_history` and is
excluded from the operational interpretation.

## Promotion gate

Do not promote the Cayley-Dickson diagnostic into a rainfall predictor from the
current issue. First build an archived monthly issue panel spanning at least ten
years and evaluate it against held-out 91103 or South Coast Drainage
precipitation. Require incremental skill over matched controls:

- Euclidean issue-to-issue displacement;
- angular change;
- cumulative temporal change;
- linear and nonlinear regressions using the same inputs;
- sign-scrambled and structure-matched associator tensors;
- ENSO-only and official-CPC-only baselines.

Use leave-year-out and event-blocked validation. Score Brier loss, log loss,
ranked probability score, calibration, above-normal tercile discrimination,
and threshold-free precipitation exceedance probabilities. Retain the official
CPC/NWS products as the operational authority until those tests succeed.
